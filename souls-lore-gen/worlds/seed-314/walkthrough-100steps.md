## Words of the Archives

**Who was Ysirveth, and what became of them?**

> Some claim Ysirveth, god of harvest, plenty, and the debt of plenty, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. Of Ysirveth, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

**Who was Quelmund, and what became of them?**

> The tellings differ, but most agree Quelmund, god of healing and the price of healing, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. Of Quelmund, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

**Who was Kamoth, and what became of them?**

> The tellings differ, but most agree Kamoth the Unmarked, last-sworn of the Order of the Veil, set out for the place where Ysirveth was lost, carrying the order's final blessing. Of Kamoth, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

**Who was Solendra, and what became of them?**

> The tellings differ, but most agree the Church of Yoryne was raised at Velothasmere; Solendra of the Long Vigil took its first pulpit, preaching that the waning is a trial and the faithful will be spared. Of Solendra, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

**Who was Serdis, and what became of them?**

> The tellings differ, but most agree at the urging of Solendra, the saint Serdis the Kindly was given to the Sundered Moon in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed. Of Serdis, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

**Who was Nyessorn, and what became of them?**

> The tellings differ, but most agree Nyessorn the Lantern-eyed united the river-clans and was crowned in Hestvethshroud; the realm took the name Belthashollow. Of Nyessorn, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

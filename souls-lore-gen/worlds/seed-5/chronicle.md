# The True Chronicle (seed 5)

> **Spoilers.** This is the ground truth the items only hint at.

**Cosmology:** the Founding Chord — gift of speech, covenant, and the resonance that holds stone to stone.
**The waning:** the Chord decays into echoes; oaths slip, bridges forget their arches.
**The curse:** the Unsinging — the voices of the afflicted fade to a hum, and what they built beside begins to loosen.

**THE VEILS (each partially true, reframed by the next; never stated by any item):**
1. The bells are not rung to honour the Chord but to drown out something singing underneath it.
2. The Chord is not fading — it is being sung backwards, deliberately, from somewhere beneath the oldest bell.
3. The backwards singer is the Chord's own first note, cast out so the harmony could begin; it does not want silence — it wants its place back.

## Ages
- **the Age of the Gift** (years 0–301): the Founding Chord gave the world speech, covenant, and the resonance that holds stone to stone.
- **the Age of Names** (years 301–525): The gods withdrew to their high seats and mortals learned to matter. Then the waning began: the Chord decays into echoes; oaths slip, bridges forget their arches.
- **the Age of Dusk** (years 525–present): The present age. The gods are silent, the roads belong to the marked, and pilgrims walk toward rumours of a cure.

## Timeline
- **Year 0** — *cosmogony*: the Founding Chord rose (or was raised — the tellings differ) and gave the world speech, covenant, and the resonance that holds stone to stone. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 4** — *claiming*: Ommoth, god of healing and the price of healing, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Athagarcradle)_
- **Year 14** — *claiming*: Nyorott, god of law, chains, and the first covenant, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Thalulgarbarrow)_
- **Year 17** — *claiming*: Sylenmoth, god of war and the keeping of thresholds, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Mazoscrown)_
  - **Year 13** — *testimony*: An account survives from Mormoth the Sleepless, a handmaid, set down against instruction and hidden in a ledger. _(at Loengarmarch)_
  - **Year 13** — *aftermath*: In the months after, singers came in unusual numbers, and left in unusual silence. _(at Loengarmarch)_
    - _Hidden:_ What they came for was not what the chronicles assumed.
- **Year 32** — *claiming*: Thaliast, god of secrets, locks, and the spaces between, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Belmundfen)_
- **Year 47** — *founding*: Ommoth founded Ishencaeldeep (church) at Athagarcradle. _(at Ishencaeldeep)_
- **Year 47** — *forging*: The Bell of Ommoth was wrought as regalia of Ommoth's throne. _(at Athagarcradle)_
- **Year 84** — *founding*: Nyorott founded Kardiscrown (kingdom) at Thalulgarbarrow. _(at Kardiscrown)_
- **Year 84** — *forging*: The Hymn of Nyorott was wrought as regalia of Nyorott's throne. _(at Thalulgarbarrow)_
- **Year 105** — *founding*: Sylenmoth founded Elaedaneshroud (kingdom) at Mazoscrown. _(at Elaedaneshroud)_
- **Year 105** — *forging*: The Hymn of Sylenmoth was wrought as regalia of Sylenmoth's throne. _(at Mazoscrown)_
- **Year 124** — *founding*: Thaliast founded Iraevethvault (kingdom) at Belmundfen. _(at Iraevethvault)_
- **Year 124** — *forging*: The Chord of Thaliast was wrought as regalia of Thaliast's throne. _(at Belmundfen)_
- **Year 177** — *great war*: Toressgrim, the Discordant, rose against the thrones. Thaliast led the gods to war. _(at Belmundfen)_
  - _Hidden:_ Sylenmoth treated with Toressgrim in secret and was spared what followed.
- **Year 182** — *sealing*: Toressgrim was defeated and sealed beneath Ysiosreach; the seal was bought with the lives of 3 champions. _(at Ysiosreach)_
- **Year 301** — *waning*: The waning began: the Chord decays into echoes; oaths slip, bridges forget their arches. Mortals called the affliction the Unsinging: the voices of the afflicted fade to a hum, and what they built beside begins to loosen. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 322** — *founding*: Draansila Half-remembered founded the Order of the Chord at Loachgate, sworn to stand against the Unsinging. _(at Loachgate)_
- **Year 336** — *founding*: The Church of Ommoth was raised at Wrenwynreach; Nimaeast the Grey took its first pulpit, preaching that the waning is a trial and the faithful will be spared. _(at Wrenwynreach)_
- **Year 351** — *heresy*: Fendis the Twice-crowned was cast out of Church of Ommoth and founded the Cult of the Patient Below, teaching that the gods themselves caused the waning. _(at Karesswyncrown)_
  - _Hidden:_ The heresy is nearer the truth than the sermon.
- **Year 365** — *founding*: Belawyn of the Last Door united the river-clans and was crowned in Mazucaelcrown; the realm took the name Ghalmundreach. _(at Mazucaelcrown)_
- **Year 393** — *rite of restoration*: At the urging of Nimaeast, the saint Sereth the Unbowed was given to the Founding Chord in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed. _(at Wrenwynreach)_
  - _Hidden:_ The rite did not restore anything. It only fed the waning more slowly — and Nimaeast suspected as much.
- **Year 423** — *betrayal*: Thalulhild the Grey, sword-hand of Belawyn, opened the gates of Mazucaelcrown to the Order of the Chord over a grievance no chronicle agrees on. _(at Mazucaelcrown)_
  - _Hidden:_ Thalulhild acted on a promise from the Cult of the Patient Below: that the marked of their house would be spared the Unsinging. The promise was not kept.
- **Year 427** — *fall of a kingdom*: Ghalmundreach fell. Belawyn of the Last Door died at the foot of their own throne; the crown was carried away and never worn again. _(at Ghalmundreach)_
- **Year 430** — *fate*: Thalulhild was found at Loachgate bearing the Unsinging, and was not spared. _(at Loachgate)_
- **Year 525** — *twilight of a god*: Sylenmoth, god of war and the keeping of thresholds, descended below the world to look upon the Founding Chord directly, and did not return. _(at Mazoscrown)_
  - _Hidden:_ Sylenmoth found the truth of the waning, and chose to stay.
- **Year 536** — *last pilgrimage*: Ulorast Last-sworn, last-sworn of the Order of the Chord, set out for the place where Sylenmoth was lost, carrying the order's final blessing. _(at Loachgate)_
- **Year 541** — *hero's end*: Word came that Ulorast fell to the marked, and then word came that Ulorast led the marked. The order believes neither. _(at Loachgate)_
  - **Year 538** — *testimony*: An account survives from Thaleeth of the Rearguard, a cartwright, set down against instruction and hidden in a coffin lid. _(at Belgarhold)_
  - **Year 540** — *aftermath*: In the months after, singers came in unusual numbers, and left in unusual silence. _(at Belgarhold)_
- **Year 560** — *the seal weakens*: Pilgrims of the Cult of the Patient Below were found digging at the seal of Toressgrim. The wardens hanged nine; the digging continued. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
  - _Hidden:_ The seal has been failing on its own since the waning began. The cult only follows the cracks.

# Codex of Found Things (seed 5)

*Of the Founding Chord little now is spoken, and less is true. Gather what the old things still remember, and be sparing with belief.*

## Armors

### Ulorast's Set

Worn armor that remembers the shape of its last bearer.

Ulorast Last-sworn, last-sworn of the Order of the Chord, set out for the place where Sylenmoth was lost, carrying the order's final blessing. It is said that word came that Ulorast fell to the marked, and then word came that Ulorast led the marked. The order believes neither. Held long enough, it suggests the waning is no accident. Perhaps it is better that the tale ends there.

*Left behind at Loachgate, and never reclaimed.*

## Catalysts

### Chord of Thaliast

A catalyst attuned to the old gift.

The Chord of Thaliast was wrought as regalia of Thaliast's throne. The name endures; little else does.

*Kept long at Belmundfen, and then kept poorly.*

## Consumables

### Warding Echo

A humble ward, spent in a moment.

Pilgrims of the Cult of the Patient Below were found digging at the seal of Toressgrim. The wardens hanged nine; the digging continued. Whether this was mercy or malice, none agree.

*Confiscated from pilgrims on the roads to the Pilgrim Roads.*

## Key Items

### Hollow Crown of Ghalmundreach

An object of no use in battle, and of great consequence.

Thalulhild the Grey, sword-hand of Belawyn, opened the gates of Mazucaelcrown to the Order of the Chord over a grievance no chronicle agrees on. None now living can say whether Ghalmundreach fell. Belawyn of the Last Door died at the foot of their own throne; the crown was carried away and never worn again. The tellings differ, but most agree Thalulhild acted on a promise from the Cult of the Patient Below: that the marked of their house would be spared the Unsinging. The promise was not kept. Held long enough, it suggests the waning is no accident. The name endures; little else does.

*Found at the foot of a throne in Ghalmundreach, beneath the dust of the banners.*

## Soul Remnants

### Remnant Soul of Saint Sereth

The lingering soul of one who would not wholly pass.

At the urging of Nimaeast, the saint Sereth the Unbowed was given to the Founding Chord in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed. The rest of the story is kept by no one.

*Kept in a reliquary at Wrenwynreach, before which the candles will not stay lit.*

### Soul of Sylenmoth

The lingering soul of one who would not wholly pass.

Sylenmoth, god of war and the keeping of thresholds, descended below the world to look upon the Founding Chord directly, and did not return. The rest of the story is kept by no one.

*Taken from a throne room at Mazoscrown where nothing else was disturbed.*

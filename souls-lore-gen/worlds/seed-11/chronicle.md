# The True Chronicle (seed 11)

> **Spoilers.** This is the ground truth the items only hint at.

**Cosmology:** the Drowned Radiance — gift of memory, tide, and the salt that keeps names from dissolving.
**The waning:** the tide recedes from the world's edges; memories thin and the drowned begin to speak.
**The curse:** the Brine-hollowing — salt blooms beneath the skin; the afflicted forget faces first, then their own.

**THE VEILS (each partially true, reframed by the next; never stated by any item):**
1. The drownings are not offerings. They are silencings — of what the drowned heard in the tide.
2. The Radiance never drowned — it was drowned, by the very powers that now pray for its return.
3. They drowned it in mercy, at its own asking: it had begun to remember the world before this one, and memory is contagious.

## Ages
- **the Age of the Gift** (years 0–344): the Drowned Radiance gave the world memory, tide, and the salt that keeps names from dissolving.
- **the Age of Names** (years 344–717): The powers withdrew to their high seats and mortals learned to matter.
- **the Age of Dusk** (years 717–present): The present age. What remains keeps the roads, and pilgrims walk toward rumours of a cure.

## Timeline
- **Year 0** — *cosmogony*: the Drowned Radiance rose (or was raised — the tellings differ) and gave the world memory, tide, and the salt that keeps names from dissolving. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 8** — *claiming*: Ysengar, god of the hunt and the mercy of endings, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Ghalorncrown)_
- **Year 33** — *founding*: Ysengar founded Verulisspire (church) at Ghalorncrown. _(at Verulisspire)_
- **Year 33** — *forging*: The Salt of Ysengar was wrought as regalia of Ysengar's throne. _(at Ghalorncrown)_
- **Year 37** — *claiming*: Olanwyn, god of harvest, plenty, and the debt of plenty, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Fenanastshroud)_
- **Year 54** — *claiming*: Mazorrion, god of storms and unkept promises, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 70** — *founding*: Olanwyn founded Galirwynvault (kingdom) at Fenanastshroud. _(at Galirwynvault)_
- **Year 70** — *forging*: The Tide of Olanwyn was wrought as regalia of Olanwyn's throne. _(at Fenanastshroud)_
- **Year 149** — *great war*: Draehild, the Still Water, rose against the thrones. Ysengar led the powers to war. _(at Ghalorncrown)_
  - _Hidden:_ Mazorrion treated with Draehild in secret and was spared what followed.
- **Year 160** — *mutual ruin*: Draehild and Ysengar destroyed one another at the war's end. Neither throne nor grave was left to either. _(at Ghalorncrown)_
  - _Hidden:_ Ysengar chose it, knowing the cost.
- **Year 175** — *claiming*: Velanoth, god of war and the keeping of thresholds, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Atherichold)_
- **Year 199** — *divine schism*: Mazorrion and Velanoth fell to war over a single domain. Velanoth kept it; Mazorrion was cast down and is named in no liturgy since. _(at Atherichold)_
  - _Hidden:_ The domain was never Velanoth's to keep.
- **Year 217** — *founding*: Velanoth founded Iriaulaspire (kingdom) at Atherichold. _(at Iriaulaspire)_
- **Year 217** — *forging*: The Conch of Velanoth was wrought as regalia of Velanoth's throne. _(at Atherichold)_
- **Year 396** — *waning*: The waning began: the tide recedes from the world's edges; memories thin and the drowned begin to speak. Mortals called the affliction the Brine-hollowing: salt blooms beneath the skin; the afflicted forget faces first, then their own. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 414** — *founding*: The Church of Olanwyn was raised at Syliagarhold; Fenach of the Last Door took its first pulpit, preaching that the waning is a trial and the faithful will be spared. _(at Syliagarhold)_
- **Year 425** — *heresy*: Vereneth of the Last Door was cast out of Verulisspire and founded the Cult of the Open Door, teaching that the powers above themselves caused the waning. _(at Verulisspire)_
  - _Hidden:_ The heresy is nearer the truth than the sermon.
- **Year 447** — *betrayal*: Isheneth the Lantern-eyed, sword-hand of Velanoth, opened the gates of Atherichold to the Cult of the Open Door over a grievance no chronicle agrees on. _(at Atherichold)_
  - _Hidden:_ Isheneth acted on a promise from the Cult of the Open Door: that the marked of their house would be spared the Brine-hollowing. The promise was not kept.
- **Year 481** — *rite of restoration*: At the urging of Fenach, the saint Omennoth of the Last Door was given to the Drowned Radiance in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed. _(at Syliagarhold)_
  - _Hidden:_ The rite did not restore anything. It only fed the waning more slowly — and Fenach suspected as much.
- **Year 483** — *fall of a kingdom*: Iriaulaspire fell. Velanoth, god of war and the keeping of thresholds, died at the foot of their own throne; the crown was carried away and never worn again. _(at Iriaulaspire)_
- **Year 487** — *fate*: Isheneth was found bearing the Brine-hollowing, and was not spared. _(at Atherichold)_
- **Year 503** — *founding*: Ranmoth the Quiet founded the Order of the Brine at Hestiaynevault, sworn to stand against the Brine-hollowing. _(at Hestiaynevault)_
- **Year 519** — *rite of restoration*: At the urging of Fenach, the saint Yoransila the Lantern-eyed was given to the Drowned Radiance in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed. _(at Syliagarhold)_
  - _Hidden:_ The rite did not restore anything. It only fed the waning more slowly — and Fenach suspected as much.
- **Year 536** — *founding*: Galulwyn the Grey united the river-clans and was crowned in Oloastreach; the realm took the name Belotthollow. _(at Oloastreach)_
- **Year 558** — *a season of plenty*: For thirty years the waning simply stopped. Orchards bore, the marked recovered, and it was called a mercy. Then it resumed, exactly where it had left off. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
  - _Hidden:_ Something was being paid during those thirty years, and the payments were recorded nowhere.
- **Year 584** — *founding*: Quelidane the Kindly united the river-clans and was crowned in Athrioncrown; the realm took the name Kawyngate. _(at Athrioncrown)_
- **Year 742** — *last pilgrimage*: Irric of the Empty Scabbard, last-sworn of the Order of the Brine, set out for the deep roads, where the affliction is said to begin, carrying the order's final blessing. _(at Hestiaynevault)_
- **Year 754** — *hero's end*: Word came that Irric fell to the marked, and then word came that Irric led them. The order believes neither. _(at Hestiaynevault)_
- **Year 793** — *twilight of a god*: Olanwyn, god of harvest, plenty, and the debt of plenty, was unmade upon their own throne. No wound was found, and no successor dared the seat. _(at Fenanastshroud)_
  - _Hidden:_ What unmade them was not violence but understanding — a truth arrived at, all at once.
- **Year 805** — *an heir returns*: Ysulmund the Quiet came to the ruin of Atherichold bearing a claim to Iriaulaspire, and was neither crowned nor turned away. _(at Atherichold)_
  - _Hidden:_ The claim is false, and Ysulmund knows it.
- **Year 819** — *the marked keep the roads*: The afflicted stopped hiding. They keep the wayhouses now, and take no toll from those already bearing the Brine-hollowing. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
  - _Hidden:_ Among them are some who were never afflicted at all.

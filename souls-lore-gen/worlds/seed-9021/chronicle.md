# The True Chronicle (seed 9021)

> **Spoilers.** This is the ground truth the items only hint at.

**Cosmology:** the First Ember — gift of warmth, disparity, and the colour of souls.
**The waning:** the Ember gutters; shadows lengthen and the dead forget to lie down.
**The curse:** the Gutter-mark — a grey brand that spreads over the heart; the marked wander until their names burn out.

**THE CENTRAL MYSTERY (never stated by any item):** The Ember is not dying — the gods siphon it to prolong their reign, and the curse is the siphon's residue.

## Ages
- **the Age of the Gift** (years 0–333): the First Ember gave the world warmth, disparity, and the colour of souls.
- **the Age of Names** (years 333–545): The gods withdrew to their high seats and mortals learned to matter. Then the waning began: the Ember gutters; shadows lengthen and the dead forget to lie down.
- **the Age of Dusk** (years 545–present): The present age. The gods are silent, the roads belong to the marked, and pilgrims walk toward rumours of a cure.

## Timeline
- **Year 0** — *cosmogony*: the First Ember rose (or was raised — the tellings differ) and gave the world warmth, disparity, and the colour of souls.
- **Year 14** — *claiming*: Aldranara, god of storms and unkept promises, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne.
- **Year 32** — *claiming*: Tormund, god of healing and the price of healing, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne.
- **Year 50** — *claiming*: Ghalanorn, god of graves and the ledger of names, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne.
- **Year 61** — *claiming*: Hestamoth, god of secrets, locks, and the spaces between, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne.
- **Year 89** — *founding*: Aldranara founded Nimiulacradle (church) at Osthildfen.
- **Year 89** — *forging*: The Ash of Aldranara was wrought as regalia of Aldranara's throne.
- **Year 127** — *founding*: Tormund founded Sylivethvault (kingdom) at Ruthiamothgate.
- **Year 127** — *forging*: The Cinder of Tormund was wrought as regalia of Tormund's throne.
- **Year 141** — *founding*: Ghalanorn founded Ysottdeep (kingdom) at Harusilamarch.
- **Year 141** — *forging*: The Ash of Ghalanorn was wrought as regalia of Ghalanorn's throne.
- **Year 172** — *founding*: Hestamoth founded Ruthgrimbarrow (kingdom) at Aldriarionreach.
- **Year 172** — *forging*: The Brand of Hestamoth was wrought as regalia of Hestamoth's throne.
- **Year 231** — *great war*: Hareast, the Cinder-Eater, rose against the thrones. Tormund led the gods to war.
  - _Hidden:_ Aldranara treated with Hareast in secret and was spared what followed.
- **Year 242** — *sealing*: Hareast was defeated and sealed beneath Queldrashroud; the seal was bought with the lives of 9 champions.
- **Year 333** — *waning*: The waning began: the Ember gutters; shadows lengthen and the dead forget to lie down. Mortals called the affliction the Gutter-mark: a grey brand that spreads over the heart; the marked wander until their names burn out.
- **Year 345** — *founding*: Morenric the Forsworn founded the Order of the Ash at Karenvanehollow, sworn to stand against the Gutter-mark.
- **Year 357** — *founding*: The Church of Ghalanorn was raised at Kadishold; Hesteyne of the Long Vigil took its first pulpit, preaching that the waning is a trial and the faithful will be spared.
- **Year 368** — *heresy*: Omirorn the Twice-crowned was cast out of Church of Ghalanorn and founded the Cult of the Patient Below, teaching that the gods themselves caused the waning.
  - _Hidden:_ The heresy is nearer the truth than the sermon.
- **Year 396** — *founding*: Quelnoth the Unbowed united the river-clans and was crowned in Banudanespire; the realm took the name Cyrvanecradle.
- **Year 420** — *rite of restoration*: At the urging of Hesteyne, the saint Morric the Adamant was given to the First Ember in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed.
  - _Hidden:_ The rite did not restore anything. It only fed the waning more slowly — and Hesteyne suspected as much.
- **Year 455** — *betrayal*: Omthas of the Long Vigil, sword-hand of Quelnoth, opened the gates of Banudanespire to the Order of the Ash over a grievance no chronicle agrees on.
  - _Hidden:_ Omthas acted on a promise from the Cult of the Patient Below: that the marked of their house would be spared the Gutter-mark. The promise was not kept.
- **Year 456** — *fall of a kingdom*: Cyrvanecradle fell. Quelnoth the Unbowed died at the foot of their own throne; the crown was carried away and never worn again.
- **Year 458** — *fate*: Omthas was found at Solucaelmarch bearing the Gutter-mark, and was not spared.
- **Year 545** — *twilight of a god*: Ghalanorn, god of graves and the ledger of names, was unmade upon their own throne. No wound was found, and no successor dared the seat.
  - _Hidden:_ What unmade the god was not violence but understanding — a truth arrived at, all at once.
- **Year 583** — *last pilgrimage*: Wrenendane the Latecomer, last-sworn of the Order of the Ash, set out for the place where Ghalanorn was lost, carrying the order's final blessing.
- **Year 591** — *hero's end*: Wrenendane was last seen at the edge of the deep roads. The order keeps a vigil that has not ended.
- **Year 613** — *the seal weakens*: Pilgrims of the Cult of the Patient Below were found digging at the seal of Hareast. The wardens hanged nine; the digging continued.
  - _Hidden:_ The seal has been failing on its own since the waning began. The cult only follows the cracks.

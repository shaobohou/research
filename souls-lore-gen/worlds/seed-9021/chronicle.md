# The True Chronicle (seed 9021)

> **Spoilers.** This is the ground truth the items only hint at.

**Cosmology:** the First Ember — gift of warmth, disparity, and the colour of souls.
**The waning:** the Ember gutters; shadows lengthen and the dead forget to lie down.
**The curse:** the Gutter-mark — a grey brand that spreads over the heart; the marked wander until their names burn out.

**THE VEILS (each partially true, reframed by the next; never stated by any item):**
1. The rites of rekindling consume souls faster than the Ember returns them, and the church keeps the arithmetic hidden.
2. The Ember is not dying — the gods siphon it to prolong their reign, and the curse is the siphon's residue.
3. The siphon was the Ember's own bargain: it feeds the gods so that something older, asleep in its heart, stays asleep. The gods know, and keep feeding.

## Ages
- **the Age of the Gift** (years 0–328): the First Ember gave the world warmth, disparity, and the colour of souls.
- **the Age of Names** (years 328–635): The gods withdrew to their high seats and mortals learned to matter. Then the waning began: the Ember gutters; shadows lengthen and the dead forget to lie down.
- **the Age of Dusk** (years 635–present): The present age. The gods are silent, the roads belong to the marked, and pilgrims walk toward rumours of a cure.

## Timeline
- **Year 0** — *cosmogony*: the First Ember rose (or was raised — the tellings differ) and gave the world warmth, disparity, and the colour of souls. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 20** — *claiming*: Yorthas, god of the hunt and the mercy of endings, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Fenulacrown)_
- **Year 24** — *claiming*: Ruthmere, god of storms and unkept promises, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Olearacradle)_
- **Year 39** — *claiming*: Mazannoth, god of healing and the price of healing, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Caliamothgate)_
- **Year 66** — *founding*: Yorthas founded Cyraricreach (kingdom) at Fenulacrown. _(at Cyraricreach)_
- **Year 66** — *forging*: The Brand of Yorthas was wrought as regalia of Yorthas's throne. _(at Fenulacrown)_
- **Year 97** — *founding*: Ruthmere founded Osthildfen (kingdom) at Olearacradle. _(at Osthildfen)_
- **Year 97** — *forging*: The Pyre of Ruthmere was wrought as regalia of Ruthmere's throne. _(at Olearacradle)_
- **Year 121** — *founding*: Mazannoth founded Elessastgate (church) at Caliamothgate. _(at Elessastgate)_
- **Year 121** — *forging*: The Cinder of Mazannoth was wrought as regalia of Mazannoth's throne. _(at Caliamothgate)_
- **Year 196** — *great war*: Eloros, the Cinder-Eater, rose against the thrones. Yorthas led the gods to war. _(at Fenulacrown)_
  - _Hidden:_ Ruthmere treated with Eloros in secret and was spared what followed.
- **Year 206** — *sealing*: Eloros was defeated and sealed beneath Hestanaramarch; the seal was bought with the lives of 7 champions. _(at Hestanaramarch)_
- **Year 328** — *waning*: The waning began: the Ember gutters; shadows lengthen and the dead forget to lie down. Mortals called the affliction the Gutter-mark: a grey brand that spreads over the heart; the marked wander until their names burn out. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 356** — *founding*: Ruthgrim the Forsworn founded the Order of the Ember at Elaegargate, sworn to stand against the Gutter-mark. _(at Elaegargate)_
- **Year 379** — *founding*: The Church of Ruthmere was raised at Hareastvault; Eloach the Twice-crowned took its first pulpit, preaching that the waning is a trial and the faithful will be spared. _(at Hareastvault)_
- **Year 403** — *heresy*: Queluveth of the Last Door was cast out of Church of Ruthmere and founded the Cult of the Open Door, teaching that the gods themselves caused the waning. _(at Wrenrionspire)_
  - _Hidden:_ The heresy is nearer the truth than the sermon.
- **Year 429** — *founding*: Mazirdis the Adamant united the river-clans and was crowned in Fenearamere; the realm took the name Morulaspire. _(at Fenearamere)_
- **Year 473** — *rite of restoration*: At the urging of Eloach, the saint Kariadane Half-remembered was given to the First Ember in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed. _(at Hareastvault)_
  - _Hidden:_ The rite did not restore anything. It only fed the waning more slowly — and Eloach suspected as much.
- **Year 511** — *betrayal*: Oloula the Lantern-eyed, sword-hand of Mazirdis, opened the gates of Fenearamere to the Order of the Ember over a grievance no chronicle agrees on. _(at Fenearamere)_
  - _Hidden:_ Oloula acted on a promise from the Cult of the Open Door: that the marked of their house would be spared the Gutter-mark. The promise was not kept.
- **Year 515** — *fall of a kingdom*: Morulaspire fell. Mazirdis the Adamant died at the foot of their own throne; the crown was carried away and never worn again. _(at Morulaspire)_
- **Year 517** — *fate*: Oloula was found at Wrenrionspire bearing the Gutter-mark, and was not spared. _(at Wrenrionspire)_
- **Year 635** — *twilight of a god*: Ruthmere, god of storms and unkept promises, took the Gutter-mark — the first of the gods to bear it — and wandered from their seat. _(at Olearacradle)_
  - _Hidden:_ The curse does not distinguish gods from mortals. It never did.
- **Year 669** — *last pilgrimage*: Banudane the Latecomer, last-sworn of the Order of the Ember, set out for the place where Ruthmere was lost, carrying the order's final blessing. _(at Elaegargate)_
- **Year 676** — *hero's end*: Word came that Banudane fell to the marked, and then word came that Banudane led the marked. The order believes neither. _(at Elaegargate)_
- **Year 683** — *the seal weakens*: Pilgrims of the Cult of the Open Door were found digging at the seal of Eloros. The wardens hanged nine; the digging continued. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
  - _Hidden:_ The seal has been failing on its own since the waning began. The cult only follows the cracks.

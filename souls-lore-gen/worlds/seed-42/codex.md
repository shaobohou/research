# Codex of Found Things (seed 42)

*Herein lie the things the dead would not put down. Read them one against another, for no keeper tells the whole of it, and the Ember keeps its own counsel.*

## Armors

### Fenrion's Set

The armour of the last-sworn of the Order of the Cinder.

Fenrion the Latecomer went to the place where Calanos was lost, bearing the order's final blessing. He returned once, said nothing, and laid his sword upon the altar. Then he walked into the dusk unarmed. The order keeps his plate as it keeps its oaths: unpolished, and without miracle. What he saw there, he did not leave behind in words.

*Left behind at Nyuastcrown, and never reclaimed.*

## Catalysts

### Pyre of Athara

Regalia of the storm-god's throne, borne by the kings of Velvethmere.

Athara raised it in the first warmth, when promises were newly made and not yet broken. The court remembers only the giving. Now the grey brand creeps upon hearts that once knelt here, and the marked wander until their names burn out — yet the halls insist the storm still keeps its word.

*Kept long at Velvethmere, and then kept poorly.*

## Consumables

### Warding Pyre

A ward-flame, blessed against what stirs below, and spent in a single kindling.

When pilgrims of the Patient Below were found digging at the seal of Galott, the wardens hanged nine, and the Church kindled these against the dark. The flame was a gift to the faithful, freely bestowed — so the pulpits say. Galott has lain sealed at Omthasdeep since the war of the gods. The digging, they add, is nothing. The digging continued.

*Confiscated from pilgrims on the roads to the Pilgrim Roads.*

## Key Items

### Hollow Crown of Bangrimhollow

A crown carried from a fallen hall and never worn again.

Torivanevault opened in the night — some say Athara himself struck the gates, that no mortal hand could have done such a thing. Banuldis the Adamant died at the foot of his own throne. The chroniclers of Bangrimhollow keep the crown hollow, and lay the ruin at a god's door, that they need not name the sword-hand who turned.

*Found at the foot of a throne in Bangrimhollow, beneath the dust of the banners.*

## Rings

### Ring of the Cinder Vigil

A vigil-ring of the Order of the Cinder.

It is said Fenrion the Latecomer wore this when he set out for the place where the god was lost — in the Age of Names, when the order was young and its altars full. He came back once and left his sword, then walked unarmed into the dusk. The ring keeps the vigil still. Its watch has outlasted the men who swore it.

*Left behind at Nyuastcrown, and never reclaimed.*

## Soul Remnants

### Remnant Soul of Saint Hariach

What remains of the saint's soul, kept in the reliquary at Karulahold.

Hariach the Saltborn gave himself freely to the Ember — so the Church tells, and so it is graven above the altar. A gift, offered with open hands, that the waning might slow a while. The faithful are taught to grieve softly and ask nothing. For a generation it held. Then the dusk returned to lengthen, and the giving was asked again.

*Kept in a reliquary at Karulahold, before which the candles will not stay lit.*

### Soul of Calanos

The soul-remnant of the covenant-god, gathered at Ruthornspire.

Calanos was first among the gods to take the grey brand, and wandered from his high seat as any marked beggar might. The court will not say it plainly, but the mark asked no leave of his godhood. Nor did the rites ever return what they were fed — a reckoning the ledgers keep, and never show. Law, it seems, binds even the lawgiver.

*Taken from a throne room at Ruthornspire where nothing else was disturbed.*

## Talismans

### Ember of Calanos

A talisman, regalia of the covenant-god's throne at Ruthornspire.

Calanos bound law to chain, and chain to the first oath. The court still keeps his ledgers, and swears the rites return as much as they cost — though none may read the sum. In the end even the lawgiver took the grey brand and wandered from his seat. What covenant, then, was left unpaid?

*Kept long at Ruthornspire, and then kept poorly.*

### Pyre of Saint Hariach

A relic-flame of the saint given to the Ember.

At Calmund's asking, Hariach the Saltborn walked into the First Ember, and for a generation the waning slowed. The Church names this mercy: a trial borne gladly, a doubt burned clean. Blessed is the one who does not ask what the fire took — nor why the slowing did not last.

*Kept in a reliquary at Karulahold, before which the candles will not stay lit.*

## Weapons

### Pyre of Calmund

A forged blade-flame, regalia of Calmund's throne at Ostachhold.

Calmund, god of craft and measure, set his fire in steel and called it just. The smiths of Nimiadragate boast that nothing his hand shaped has ever failed. They do not speak of the grey mark, which measures every heart alike and asks no leave of the forge.

*Kept long at Ostachhold, and then kept poorly.*

### Athara's War-Brand

A war-blade kindled for the god's own hand.

When Galott the Cinder-Eater rose against the thrones, Athara led the gods to war, and the brand drank deep at Omthasdeep. Five champions bought the seal with their lives. It is said Calanos was spared the worst of that field, though the tellings differ on why. The proud halls name it victory, and leave the rest unspoken.

*Worn smooth by warders' hands at Omthasdeep.*

### Ishencael's Oathbreaker Blade

The blade of the sword-hand who opened the gates.

Ishencael the Adamant let the Order of the Cinder into Torivanevault, over a grievance no chronicle names alike. The king fell — or so it was told; some swear Banuldis was borne off living, and waits yet in some hollow for the crown to be returned. The blade keeps its own counsel. It remembers a gate, and a promise, and neither fondly.

*Found at the foot of a throne in Bangrimhollow, beneath the dust of the banners.*

# The True Chronicle (seed 42)

> **Spoilers.** This is the ground truth the items only hint at.

**Cosmology:** the First Ember — gift of warmth, disparity, and the colour of souls.
**The waning:** the Ember gutters; shadows lengthen and the dead forget to lie down.
**The curse:** the Gutter-mark — a grey brand that spreads over the heart; the marked wander until their names burn out.

**THE VEILS (each partially true, reframed by the next; never stated by any item):**
1. The rites of rekindling consume souls faster than the Ember returns them, and the church keeps the arithmetic hidden.
2. The Ember is not dying — the gods siphon it to prolong their reign, and the curse is the siphon's residue.
3. The siphon was the Ember's own bargain: it feeds the gods so that something older, asleep in its heart, stays asleep. The gods know, and keep feeding.

## Ages
- **the Age of the Gift** (years 0–249): the First Ember gave the world warmth, disparity, and the colour of souls.
- **the Age of Names** (years 249–453): The gods withdrew to their high seats and mortals learned to matter. Then the waning began: the Ember gutters; shadows lengthen and the dead forget to lie down.
- **the Age of Dusk** (years 453–present): The present age. The gods are silent, the roads belong to the marked, and pilgrims walk toward rumours of a cure.

## Timeline
- **Year 0** — *cosmogony*: the First Ember rose (or was raised — the tellings differ) and gave the world warmth, disparity, and the colour of souls. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 20** — *claiming*: Athara, god of storms and unkept promises, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Velvethmere)_
- **Year 23** — *claiming*: Calmund, god of craft, measure, and the forge, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Ostachhold)_
- **Year 26** — *claiming*: Calanos, god of law, chains, and the first covenant, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. _(at Ruthornspire)_
- **Year 53** — *founding*: Athara founded Ghalorhildfen (kingdom) at Velvethmere. _(at Ghalorhildfen)_
- **Year 53** — *forging*: The Pyre of Athara was wrought as regalia of Athara's throne. _(at Velvethmere)_
- **Year 71** — *founding*: Calmund founded Nimiadragate (kingdom) at Ostachhold. _(at Nimiadragate)_
- **Year 71** — *forging*: The Pyre of Calmund was wrought as regalia of Calmund's throne. _(at Ostachhold)_
- **Year 110** — *founding*: Calanos founded Hestirosshroud (kingdom) at Ruthornspire. _(at Hestirosshroud)_
- **Year 110** — *forging*: The Ember of Calanos was wrought as regalia of Calanos's throne. _(at Ruthornspire)_
- **Year 154** — *great war*: Galott, the Cinder-Eater, rose against the thrones. Athara led the gods to war. _(at Velvethmere)_
  - _Hidden:_ Calanos treated with Galott in secret and was spared what followed.
- **Year 163** — *sealing*: Galott was defeated and sealed beneath Omthasdeep; the seal was bought with the lives of 5 champions. _(at Omthasdeep)_
- **Year 249** — *waning*: The waning began: the Ember gutters; shadows lengthen and the dead forget to lie down. Mortals called the affliction the Gutter-mark: a grey brand that spreads over the heart; the marked wander until their names burn out. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 267** — *founding*: Ishveth the Adamant founded the Order of the Cinder at Nyuastcrown, sworn to stand against the Gutter-mark. _(at Nyuastcrown)_
- **Year 289** — *founding*: The Church of Athara was raised at Karulahold; Ranach the Kindly took its first pulpit, preaching that the waning is a trial and the faithful will be spared. _(at Karulahold)_
- **Year 306** — *heresy*: Fenenyne the Quiet was cast out of Church of Athara and founded the Cult of the Patient Below, teaching that the gods themselves caused the waning. _(at Thalmerecrown)_
  - _Hidden:_ The heresy is nearer the truth than the sermon.
- **Year 330** — *founding*: Banuldis the Adamant united the river-clans and was crowned in Torivanevault; the realm took the name Bangrimhollow. _(at Torivanevault)_
- **Year 347** — *rite of restoration*: At the urging of Ranach, the saint Hariach the Saltborn was given to the First Ember in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed. _(at Karulahold)_
  - _Hidden:_ The rite did not restore anything. It only fed the waning more slowly — and Ranach suspected as much.
- **Year 386** — *betrayal*: Ishencael the Adamant, sword-hand of Banuldis, opened the gates of Torivanevault to the Order of the Cinder over a grievance no chronicle agrees on. _(at Torivanevault)_
  - _Hidden:_ Ishencael acted on a promise from the Cult of the Patient Below: that the marked of their house would be spared the Gutter-mark. The promise was not kept.
- **Year 389** — *fall of a kingdom*: Bangrimhollow fell. Banuldis the Adamant died at the foot of their own throne; the crown was carried away and never worn again. _(at Bangrimhollow)_
- **Year 399** — *fate*: Ishencael was found at Nyuastcrown bearing the Gutter-mark, and was not spared. _(at Nyuastcrown)_
- **Year 453** — *twilight of a god*: Calanos, god of law, chains, and the first covenant, took the Gutter-mark — the first of the gods to bear it — and wandered from their seat. _(at Ruthornspire)_
  - _Hidden:_ The curse does not distinguish gods from mortals. It never did.
- **Year 487** — *last pilgrimage*: Fenrion the Latecomer, last-sworn of the Order of the Cinder, set out for the place where Calanos was lost, carrying the order's final blessing. _(at Nyuastcrown)_
- **Year 494** — *hero's end*: Fenrion returned once, said nothing, left their sword upon the order's altar, and walked into the dusk unarmed. _(at Nyuastcrown)_
- **Year 504** — *the seal weakens*: Pilgrims of the Cult of the Patient Below were found digging at the seal of Galott. The wardens hanged nine; the digging continued. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
  - _Hidden:_ The seal has been failing on its own since the waning began. The cult only follows the cracks.

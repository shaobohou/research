# Codex of Found Things (seed 9)

*Of the Drowned Radiance little now is spoken, and less is true. Gather what the old things still remember, and be sparing with belief.*

## Armors

### Mazamund's Set

Worn armor that remembers the shape of its last bearer.

Mazamund the Unmarked, last-sworn of the Order of the Brine, set out for the place where Sylorien was lost, carrying the order's final blessing. The tellings differ, but most agree Mazamund returned once, said nothing, left their sword upon the order's altar, and walked into the dusk unarmed. What became of it after is not written.

*Left behind at Olulthasshroud, and never reclaimed.*

## Consumables

### Warding Salt

A humble ward, spent in a moment.

Pilgrims of the Cult of the Second Dawn were found digging at the seal of Ishirula. The wardens hanged nine; the digging continued. Perhaps it is better that the tale ends there.

*Confiscated from pilgrims on the roads to the Pilgrim Roads.*

## Key Items

### Hollow Crown of Hestnothgate

An object of no use in battle, and of great consequence.

Hestirdane the Grey, sword-hand of Ezric, opened the gates of Quelwynbarrow to the Order of the Brine over a grievance no chronicle agrees on. The tellings differ, but most agree Hestirdane acted on a promise from the Cult of the Second Dawn: that the marked of their house would be spared the Brine-hollowing. The promise was not kept. Whether this was mercy or malice, none agree.

*Found at the foot of a throne in Hestnothgate, beneath the dust of the banners.*

## Soul Remnants

### Remnant Soul of Saint Yormere

The lingering soul of one who would not wholly pass.

### Soul of Sylorien

The lingering soul of one who would not wholly pass.

### Tide of Saint Yormere

A talisman for the invoking of half-forgotten rites.

At the urging of Thalenien, the saint Yormere the Adamant was given to the Drowned Radiance in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed. None now living can say whether the rite did not restore anything. It only fed the waning more slowly — and Thalenien suspected as much. Held long enough, it suggests the waning is no accident. Perhaps it is better that the tale ends there.

*Kept in a reliquary at Ostenthasvault, before which the candles will not stay lit.*

## Weapons

### Nimos's War-Brine

A weapon of an older make, still keen despite its years.

Ishirula, the Still Water, rose against the thrones. Nimos led the gods to war. None now living can say whether Ishirula was defeated and sealed beneath Aldranarahollow; the seal was bought with the lives of 9 champions. What became of it after is not written.

*Worn smooth by warders' hands at Aldranarahollow.*

### Hestirdane's Oathbreaker Blade

A weapon of an older make, still keen despite its years.

What became of it after is not written.

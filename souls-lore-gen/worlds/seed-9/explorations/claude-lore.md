## Accounts Unearthed

### On the trail of Tide of Saint Yormere

- *(year 405, the choosing)* The saint was not the first choice. The first choice ran, and was let run; the chronicles kept the saint's serenity and lost the running.
- *(year 408, the procession)* The procession took the long road, through every village, so that all might see what their deliverance cost. Veremis walked at the saint's left hand and never afterward spoke of it.
- **Brought back:** Processional Conch (talisman)

### On the trail of Ishirula

- *(year 178, battle)* The war's worst day was at Seriaachvault, where the line held for a night and a morning under Solemis of the Rearguard, and then did not hold.
- *(year 180, champion's duel)* Before the last assault, champions met between the hosts, as the old law required. What was said there was not recorded; what was done there decided the war.
- **Brought back:** Torn Standard of Seriaachvault (talisman)

### On the trail of Ishirula

- *(year 175, testimony)* An account survives from Ghalorien the Stray, a siege captain, set down against instruction and hidden in a wall.
- *(year 175, aftermath)* In the months after, singers came in unusual numbers, and left in unusual silence.

### On the trail of Mazamund

- *(year 543, testimony)* An account survives from Ulesssila Thrice-pardoned, a chronicler, set down against instruction and hidden in a psalter.
- *(year 544, aftermath)* In the months after, singers came in unusual numbers, and left in unusual silence.

### On the trail of War-Brine

- *(year 194, the paying of the price)* Of the champions spent on the seal, most are a list now; two are still prayed to by name — Ezahild Long-memoried and Ezlis the Sleepless — though none can say what distinguishes the remembered dead from the rest.
- *(year 194, the wardens' charge)* A wardenship was sworn over the seal, hereditary and unthanked. Its rolls have never once been complete.
- **Brought back:** Warden's Salt-ring (ring)

## Words of the Archives

**Who was Thalenien, who urged the rite of restoration?**

> Some claim the Church of Veloryne was raised at Ostenthasvault; Thalenien the Unbowed took its first pulpit, preaching that the waning is a trial and the faithful will be spared. Of Thalenien, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

**What did Mazamund find in the place where Sylorien was lost?**

> None now living can say whether Sylorien, god of law, chains, and the first covenant, claimed a portion of the gift and took a throne. Of Sylorien, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

**Who was Veremis, who walked at the saint's left hand?**

> The tellings differ, but most agree the saint was not the first choice. The first choice ran, and was let run; the chronicles kept the saint's serenity and lost the running. Of Veremis, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

**What became of the wardens who keep the seal of Ishirula?**

> Some claim Ishirula, the Still Water, rose against the thrones. Nimos led the gods to war. Of Ishirula, the records otherwise keep their counsel.
>     — a marginal note, unsigned

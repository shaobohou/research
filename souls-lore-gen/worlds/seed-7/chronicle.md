# The True Chronicle (seed 7)

> **Spoilers.** This is the ground truth the items only hint at.

**Cosmology:** the Pale Root — gift of order, season, and the slow sap that binds moments in sequence.
**The waning:** the Root greys and splits; hours repeat, orchards fruit with last year's fruit.
**The curse:** the Ringing-rot — growth rings surface on the flesh of the afflicted, one for each hour they relive.

**THE VEILS (each partially true, reframed by the next; never stated by any item):**
1. The pruning-hooks of the church are not symbols; something is still cut from the Root each season, and burned unseen.
2. The Root grew from a buried corpse, and the order it grants is that corpse's refusal to be forgotten.
3. The corpse is not dead but dreaming in sequence; the seasons are its heartbeats, and the rot began when it started to wake.

## Ages
- **the Age of the Gift** (years 0–282): the Pale Root gave the world order, season, and the slow sap that binds moments in sequence.
- **the Age of Names** (years 282–519): The gods withdrew to their high seats and mortals learned to matter. Then the waning began: the Root greys and splits; hours repeat, orchards fruit with last year's fruit.
- **the Age of Dusk** (years 519–present): The present age. The gods are silent, the roads belong to the marked, and pilgrims walk toward rumours of a cure.

- **Year 330** — *heresy*: Belwyn the Unbowed was cast out of Church of Queliamund and founded the Cult of the Patient Below, teaching that the gods themselves caused the waning. _(at Kaemisvault)_

  - **Year 329** — *testimony*: An account survives from Hesticael Long-memoried, a handmaid, set down against instruction and hidden in a ledger. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
  - **Year 329** — *aftermath*: In the months after, crows came in unusual numbers, and left in unusual silence. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
- **Year 349** — *founding*: Ostorthas the Grey united the river-clans and was crowned in Galomerebarrow; the realm took the name Olagarcradle. _(at Galomerebarrow)_
- **Year 393** — *rite of restoration*: At the urging of Belwyn, the saint Caliadis of the Long Vigil was given to the Pale Root in the rite of restoration. For a generation, the waning slowed. _(at Kaemisvault)_
  - _Hidden:_ The rite did not restore anything. It only fed the waning more slowly — and Belwyn suspected as much.
  - **Year 390** — *the choosing*: The saint was not the first choice. The first choice ran, and was let run; the chronicles kept the saint's serenity and lost the running. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
    - _Hidden:_ The one who ran was never pursued, by design.
  - **Year 393** — *the procession*: The procession took the long road, through every village, so that all might see what their deliverance cost. Rutheemis walked at the saint's left hand and never afterward spoke of it. _(at Kaemisvault)_
    - **Year 387** — *testimony*: An account survives from Belordra Cinder-shod, a witness, set down against instruction and hidden in a coffin lid. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
    - **Year 390** — *aftermath*: In the months after, pilgrims came in unusual numbers, and left in unusual silence. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
      - _Hidden:_ What they came for was not what the chronicles assumed.
- **Year 439** — *betrayal*: Wrensila the Quiet, sword-hand of Ostorthas, opened the gates of Galomerebarrow to the Order of the Graft over a grievance no chronicle agrees on. _(at Galomerebarrow)_
  - _Hidden:_ Wrensila acted on a promise from the Cult of the Patient Below: that the marked of their house would be spared the Ringing-rot. The promise was not kept.
- **Year 443** — *fall of a kingdom*: Olagarcradle fell. Ostorthas the Grey died at the foot of their own throne; the crown was carried away and never worn again. _(at Olagarcradle)_
- **Year 450** — *fate*: Wrensila was found at Kaemisvault bearing the Ringing-rot, and was not spared. _(at Kaemisvault)_
- **Year 519** — *twilight of a god*: Ghalenmund, god of secrets, locks, and the spaces between, descended below the world to look upon the Pale Root directly, and did not return. _(at Toromundmarch)_
  - _Hidden:_ Ghalenmund found the truth of the waning, and chose to stay.
- **Year 534** — *last pilgrimage*: Eloric the Unmarked, last-sworn of the Order of the Graft, set out for the place where Ghalenmund was lost, carrying the order's final blessing. _(at Ulirwyncradle)_
- **Year 546** — *hero's end*: Eloric was last seen at the edge of the deep roads. The order keeps a vigil that has not ended. _(at Ulirwyncradle)_
- **Year 559** — *the seal weakens*: Pilgrims of the Cult of the Patient Below were found digging at the seal of Iriorn. The wardens hanged nine; the digging continued. _(at the Pilgrim Roads)_
  - _Hidden:_ The seal has been failing on its own since the waning began. The cult only follows the cracks.
